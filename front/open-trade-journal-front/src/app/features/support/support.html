01 How do I import my trading history? expand_more

The Import page is the starting point for populating Trade Journal with your trading history. It allows you to import trade execution data from a supported broker using a CSV file.

02 How does the Trading Dashboard work? expand_more

The Trading Dashboard provides a quick overview of your trading performance and activity for a selected period. It combines performance statistics with a cumulative P&L chart so you can quickly understand how your trading performed over time.

DATE FILTER

The filter at the top of the page controls the period used to calculate the dashboard statistics and performance chart. Changing the filter allows you to analyze a specific trading period instead of looking at your entire trading history.

IMPORTANT The selected date range determines which trades are included in the dashboard calculations. Use the filter whenever you want to compare performance across different periods.
PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS

The Performance highlights section summarizes the most important results for the selected period.

Total P&L
Shows the total realized profit or loss generated by the trades included in the selected period.
Win Rate
Shows the percentage of trades that were profitable during the selected period.
Total Trades
Shows the number of trades included in the selected period.
Best Trade
Shows the trade with the highest P&L during the selectedperiod.
Worst Trade
Shows the trade with the lowest P&L during the selectedperiod.
Best Day
Shows the trading day with the highest total P&L.
Worst Day
Shows the trading day with the lowest total P&L.
P&L PERFORMANCE

The P&L Performance panel displays the cumulative performance of the trades within the selected period.
The chart helps visualize how your P&L developed throughout the selected date range. Positive and negative movements make it easier to identify periods of strong performance or drawdown.

Selected trades Daily P&L Cumulative P&L
USE CASES
  • Review your overall performance for a specific trading period.
  • Compare your performance across different date ranges.
  • Quickly identify your best and worst trades.
  • Identify your most profitable and least profitable trading days.
  • Monitor your win rate and total number of trades.
  • Analyze the evolution of your cumulative P&L.
  • Use a custom date range to focus on a specific trading period.
RECOMMENDED Start with a broad period to understand your overall performance, then use the date filter to investigate specific weeks, months, or trading periods where your performance changed significantly.
03 How does the Trading Analysis page work? expand_more

The Trading Analysis page provides a deeper view of your trading results. It allows you to analyze how your P&L is distributed over time and identify which stocks contributed most to your performance.

DATE FILTER

The filter at the top of the page determines which trades are included in the analysis. You can select a predefined period or use a custom date range to focus on a specific part of your trading history.

IMPORTANT The selected date range applies to the analysis shown on this page. Changing the filter allows you to analyze different trading periods without changing your underlying trade history.
DAILY P&L HISTORY

The Daily P&L history chart displays the profit and loss generated during the selected period.
Each bar represents the P&L for a specific time group. Positive results are displayed above the zero line, while negative results are displayed below it.

DAY
Displays P&L grouped by individual trading days.
WEEK
Groups the P&L by trading week to make weeklyperformance easier to compare.
MONTH
Groups the P&L by month to identify monthly performancetrends.
YEAR
Groups the P&L by year for a broader performance view.
ANALYSIS TIP Use DAY when investigating individual trading sessions, WEEK or MONTH when looking for performance trends, and YEAR when comparing longer-term results.
STOCK RANKING

The Stock ranking section identifies the best performing stocks within the selected period.

WHAT CAN YOU LEARN?
  • Identify profitable and unprofitable trading days.
  • Identify weekly, monthly, and yearly P&L trends.
  • Compare performance across different time periods.
  • Find the stocks that contributed most to your overall P&L.
  • Identify which stocks generated losses during the selected period.
  • Understand how your trading performance is distributed across different stocks and time periods.
USE CASES
01 Review daily performance

Use the DAY view to investigate individual profitable and losing trading sessions.

02 Find performance trends

Switch to WEEK or MONTH to identify broader patterns in your trading results.

03 Analyze stock performance

Use Stock ranking to determine which symbols contributed most to your P&L.

04 Investigate a specific period

Use the custom date range to focus on a specific trading period and analyze its results.

RECOMMENDED Start with the DAY view to understand individual trading sessions. Then switch to WEEK or MONTH to identify broader patterns and use Stock ranking to determine which stocks are driving your performance.
04 How does the Trade Trace page work? expand_more

The Trade Trace page allows you to follow your trading activity over time and inspect individual trades in detail. It combines a trading calendar, annual activity overview, trade history, and trade evaluation tools in one place.

TRADING CALENDAR

The Trading Calendar provides a monthly view of your trading activity. Each day indicates whether you had profitable trades, losing trades, or no trading activity.
Use the previous and next month buttons to navigate through your trading history month by month.

USE CASE The calendar is useful for quickly identifying profitable and losing trading periods and understanding how consistently you were active throughout the month.
MONTHLY STATISTICS

The Monthly Statistics panel summarizes the performance of the currently selected month.

Best Trade
Shows the highest-performing trade recorded during theselected month.
Worst Trade
Shows the lowest-performing trade recorded during theselected month.
Best Day
Shows the most profitable trading day of the selected month.
Worst Day
Shows the worst-performing trading day of the selected month.
Win Rate
Shows the percentage of profitable trading results for theselected month.
Total Win / Total Loss
Shows the total positive and negative P&L for theselected month.
TRADING ACTIVITY

The Trading Activity section provides a year-level overview of your trading activity.
Each month is represented by a sequence of daily activity indicators. The indicators distinguish between days with no trades, profitable activity, and losing activity.
Use the previous year and next year buttons to inspect activity from different years.

ANNUAL TRADING SUMMARY

The Annual Trading Summary gives you a compact overview of the selected year's activity.

Traded Days
Number of days on which trading activity was recorded.
No Trade Days
Number of days without recorded trading activity.
Green Days
Number of profitable trading days.
Red Days
Number of losing trading days.
Green Day Rate
Percentage of traded days that were profitable.
TRADE HISTORY

The Trade History section contains the individual trading records imported into the journal. This is where you can move from a high-level view to a specific trade.
The symbol filter allows you to search for trades associated with a particular stock or symbol.

Enter symbol Filter trades Inspect trade Evaluate

Use Reset to remove the symbol filter and return to the complete trade history.

TRADE EVALUATION

Each trade can be evaluated individually using the Comment button in the Evaluation column. Clicking the button opens the Trade Evaluation dialog for that specific trade.

Select trade Comment Trade Evaluation

The evaluation dialog is designed to help you review the quality of your trading decision rather than only looking at the financial result.

HOW THE EVALUATION DIALOG WORKS

When the evaluation dialog opens, the selected trade is shown at the top so you can immediately confirm which trade you are reviewing. The dialog displays information such as the symbol, side, quantity, and trade price.

Positive Points

Add the things you did well in the trade, such as good timing, good risk management, or following your trading plan.

Negative Points

Add mistakes or weaknesses in the trade, such as poor timing, excessive risk, or breaking your trading rules.

Comment

Write a more detailed explanation of the trade and describe what happened or what you learned from it.

Screenshots

Attach screenshots related to the trade, for example charts or other visual references that help explain the trading decision.

WHY EVALUATE TRADES?

Trade evaluation turns your historical trades into a learning record. Instead of only knowing whether a trade made or lost money, you can record why the trade was good or bad and identify patterns in your decision-making.

  • Record what was done correctly.
  • Identify mistakes and weaknesses.
  • Document the reasoning behind a trade.
  • Keep screenshots and visual context for later review.
  • Build a personal record of recurring trading behaviors.
  • Use previous evaluations to improve future trading decisions.
